EN_TITLE: Intro to Hostility
RU_TITLE: 

EN_SUBTITLE: Artifacts of Endless War
RU_SUBTITLE: 

EN_DESCRIPTION:
Throughout your journey, you will discover that the world itself adapts to your growing strength. As you conquer dungeons, defeat mighty foes, and amass powerful equipment, the realm becomes increasingly hostile.

Monsters will begin to manifest powerful &dAbilities&f, ranging from Common traits to Advanced and even Legendary powers. Some will reflect your attacks, regenerate from near death, summon allies, or unleash devastating effects that can overwhelm even seasoned adventurers.

These empowered creatures are known as Champions. Though far deadlier than ordinary monsters, they also guard the greatest treasures.

Fortunately, the world offers a way to fight back.

Hidden within dangerous dungeons, rare loot, and the hands of formidable Champions are legendary &6Artifacts&f , ancient relics forged to counter specific &dAbilities&f. Each artifact weakens or completely negates a particular Champion power, allowing you to turn impossible battles into winnable ones.

=== [КВЕСТ 3 | ID: 5A2FA9E3C013A435] ===
EN_TITLE: 
RU_TITLE: 

EN_SUBTITLE: Detecting Danger
RU_SUBTITLE: 

EN_DESCRIPTION:
&4The Hostility Detector &fis your most valuable survival tool. When held, it displays both your Personal Hostility and the Regional Hostility of the area around you.

Personal Hostility reflects your own progression through the world. As you grow stronger and conquer greater challenges, increasingly dangerous enemies will begin to appear.

Regional Hostility measures how dangerous the current area has become. Regions with high Hostility spawn stronger Champions with deadlier Abilities, but they also offer better loot, rarer Artifacts, and greater rewards.

Before venturing into unfamiliar lands, always check your Hostility Detector. Understanding the danger ahead is often the difference between victory and death.

RU_DESCRIPTION:
